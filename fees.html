Payment rules

  • Twelve months, no deductions. School fees cover twelve calendar months and may be paid in monthly instalments or in advance. No deductions are made for holidays or partial attendance. Fees once paid are non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • Due by the 18th. Monthly fees must be paid by the 18th of each month. Late payment incurs a late fee of Rs 50 for the current month and an additional Rs 50 for every subsequent month of delay, calculated cumulatively.
  • Delay has consequences. Delay in payment may result in dismissal. If the delay exceeds two months, the student will be sent back home without further notice.
  • Holiday months in advance. Fees for holiday months must be paid in advance before the school closes for the holidays. All dues must be cleared before the commencement of the annual examinations.
  • Online only. Fees are paid online only, through the "School Canvas, Don Bosco School Oodlabari" app. Cash transactions are strictly prohibited.
  • Revisions possible. The school reserves the right to revise the fee structure at any time during the academic year. A 10% annual increase in school fees is customary.
  • Fee counter hours. The fee counter is open on all school days from 8:00 am to 12:00 noon for any queries.

For fee queries, refer to the fee book, or contact the school office. See Contact.
