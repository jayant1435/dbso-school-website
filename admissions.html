Admissions 2027–28

Applications are open for UKG and Class XI only, per the school admission notice dated 24 September 2026.

Forms close
10 December 2026
Entrance, interaction and counselling
17 and 18 December 2026
Merit list
18 December 2026

How to apply

  1. Get the form

    Forms are available offline at the school office, 9:00 am to 1:00 pm on working days, and online. The form fee is Rs 1,000, non-refundable.

  2. Submit by 10 December 2026

    Submit the completed form with the documents listed below, on or before 10 December 2026. Fill the form with utmost accuracy: no changes are permitted later, and the school does not accept affidavits for changes in the date of birth once entered in the school records.

  3. Entrance, interaction and counselling

    On 17 and 18 December 2026. The entrance examination has three papers: English, Mathematics, and a third paper. The pattern of the examination is shared after registration. Candidates are examined on the syllabus of the class immediately below the one they are applying for. Only selected applicants are called for the examination, and only those who pass it are admitted. New candidates must be introduced personally to the Principal by the person who will be responsible for their conduct and fees.

  4. Merit list

    The merit list is published on 18 December 2026.

  5. Admission confirmed

    Class XI admission is provisional until Board results and the original mark sheet are submitted. The new session starts in April 2027. The Rector or Principal is the final authority in granting admissions, and the decision is final and binding.

Who can apply

  • UKG. The child must have completed 5 years on or before 31 March 2027.
  • Class XI Science. Minimum 80% aggregate in the Class X board examination.
  • Class XI Commerce. Minimum 70% aggregate in the Class X board examination.
  • Class XI Humanities. Minimum 60% aggregate in the Class X board examination.
  • Admission to other classes is considered only against chance vacancies at the beginning of the school year.
  • No fresh admission is given in Classes X and XII, except in exceptional cases, such as a transfer from another ICSE or ISC school.
  • Re-admission to the school is possible only after meeting the Principal with the transfer certificate.
  • The ISC course is normally reserved for students who completed ICSE at Don Bosco School Oodlabari. Admission is at the discretion of the school authorities, based on academic and non-academic performance, including conduct.

Documents needed

For UKG applications, keep these ready with the form:

  • The completed admission form and payment receipt.
  • The birth certificate of the candidate.
  • Aadhaar cards of the candidate and both parents.
  • One postcard-size family photograph.
  • Three passport-size photographs of the ward.

For candidates who attended a recognised school, a Transfer Certificate from the previous school is required. Catholic students are also required to provide a Baptism certificate.

Uniform

Uniform is mandatory, clean and complete every day, and parents are responsible for it. A child in improper uniform may be fined or sent home. Hair is kept neat, with no funky styles, and boys are clean-shaven.

The school sells the uniform items itself: the DB Badge, DB Monogram, Maroon Tie, Grey Belt, Grey Trousers, Shorts and Skirts, Grey Cap, Muffler and Activity Dress.

On PT days, children wear white canvas keds. On Fridays, they wear the Activity Dress.

Boys

  • UKG to Class 4: grey shorts; Class 5 upwards: grey trousers.
  • White half-sleeve shirt with the school emblem, maroon tie and grey belt.
  • Black leather shoes and white socks.
  • In winter: grey cardigan, and a navy blazer with the emblem from Class 4 upwards, with grey cap and muffler.

Girls

  • Grey skirts with white slacks.
  • White short-sleeve shirt with the school emblem, maroon tie and grey belt, with white ribbons.
  • Black leather shoes and white socks.
  • In winter: grey cardigan, and a navy blazer with the emblem from Class 4 upwards, with grey cap and muffler.

School rules at a glance

Every student keeps a School Diary, the official record of academic performance and conduct, with name and class entered correctly.

Regularity

  • Be punctual: reach school at least five minutes before assembly.
  • More than 15 days absent without authorisation, and the name may be removed from the rolls.
  • Illness of two days or more needs a doctor's certificate.
  • No leave for birthdays, weddings or festivals — leave for such occasions is not granted.
  • Be present on the first day after a vacation and the last day before it.
  • Attendance on compulsory-attendance days is mandatory; defaulters pay a fine of Rs 1,000 and must seek readmission.

Conduct

  • Be polite and respectful to everyone.
  • No electronic gadgets in school.
  • No weapons in school.
  • No ragging or bullying, no violence, no abusive language.
  • No tobacco, alcohol or harmful substances.
  • No cheating or malpractice in examinations.
  • No damage to school property — damage is charged for.
  • Early leaving is allowed only in an emergency or for medical reasons.
