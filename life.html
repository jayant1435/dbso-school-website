Life at Bosco

Sport, clubs, houses and celebrations. The school year at Don Bosco Oodlabari is full, and the photographs here are the school's own.

Sport

Four houses, Red, Blue, Green and Yellow, compete through the year, and the biennial Sports Day is one of the biggest days on the calendar.

  • Football
  • Cricket
  • Volleyball
  • Basketball
  • Kabaddi
  • Badminton
  • Carrom
  • Chess
  • Table tennis
  • Sports Day

Sports Day also has lighter contests — Musical Chair and Bursting of Balloons.

Clubs

The school runs 20 clubs. A child who loves art, or code, or the outdoors, or the stage, will find a place here.

  • Literary
  • Debating
  • Science
  • Maths
  • Computer
  • Nature
  • Social Service
  • Karate
  • Taekwondo
  • Yoga
  • Aerobics
  • Gymnastics
  • Football
  • Cricket

  • Kabaddi

  • Music

Celebrations and events

  • Bosco Fizza, the inter-school fest
  • Bosco Jayanti, the feast of St. John Bosco
  • Investiture ceremony
  • Ethnic Day and Children's Day Fete
  • Rabindra Jayanti, Spell Bee, debate and elocution
  • Study tours and industrial visits — annual tours to historical and cultural landmarks, with field and industrial visits built into the curriculum
  • Independence Day, Republic Day, Teachers' Day, Parents' Day, Sports Day, Christmas and New Year

Beyond the classroom

Co-curricular life runs from public speaking and elocution in four languages to quiz, theatre, spelling bees, art and craft, and seminars.

  • Public Speaking
  • Elocution — English, Hindi, Nepali, Bengali
  • Quiz
  • Extempore Speech
  • Debates
  • Theatre and Drama
  • Spelling Bee
  • Poem Recitation
  • Storytelling
  • Drawing and Painting
  • Art & Craft
  • Music and Choir
  • Folk Songs
  • Dance
  • Scouts & Guides
  • Seminars
